Tile fitting guide

01

Fitting procedure

Small-format tiles · 1–3 ft

A careful, consistent layout helps smaller tiles create a neat finish with even joints.

    A cost-conscious approach

    Grout joints made for lasting performance.

    For some wide or deep floor and wall joints, a cement-based grout may be suitable as a backing or filler, with epoxy grout finishing the remaining joint. This can help manage material costs while keeping epoxy where it is needed.

    Check the product instructions first: a 3 mm backing is only a guide, not a universal specification. Joint depth, preparation and compatibility must follow the epoxy manufacturer’s requirements. Do not simply coat cured cement grout with a thin layer of epoxy unless the product system specifically allows it.
